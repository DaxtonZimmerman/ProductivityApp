Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

About this policy

Student Productivity helps people organize tasks, notes, and calendar events. This policy explains what information the app may access and how it is used.

Information we access

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app may access your Google account identity and the calendar events you authorize it to access. We use this information only to display, create, update, or delete calendar events at your request.

The app may also process tasks and settings that you enter. We do not request your Google password.

How we use information

We use authorized information to provide the app’s productivity and calendar features, maintain account access, respond to support requests, and protect the service. We do not sell your personal information or use Google Calendar data for advertising.

Sharing and storage

We do not share your personal information with third parties except service providers needed to operate the app, such as Firebase and Google APIs, or when required by law. Information is retained only as long as needed to provide the service or as required for security and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can stop calendar access at any time by disconnecting your Google account in the app or removing Student Productivity’s access from your Google Account security settings. You may also contact us to request help with your account information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Student Productivity project owner through the support email listed in the app’s Google consent screen.
